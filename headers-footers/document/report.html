Summary

Orders grew in all five regions in the third quarter. The largest growth came from the two regions that opened a local warehouse in June. Delivery times fell, and support tickets per order also fell.

Two incidents stopped order intake for a short time. Both had a fix on the same day. The next sections give the numbers for each region, the incidents and the plan for the fourth quarter.

Incidents

On 14 August, a certificate expired on the payment gateway. Order intake stopped for 38 minutes. The team renewed the certificate and added an alert 30 days before each expiry date.

On 22 September, a database migration locked the orders table. Order intake was slow for 12 minutes. Migrations now run in smaller steps, outside the busy hours.

Plan for the fourth quarter

The South region opens its local warehouse in October. The target is a delivery time under 3 days in every region by the end of December. The support team adds weekend hours for the holiday weeks.

Each month, the board gets a short update with the orders, the delivery times and the open incidents.
